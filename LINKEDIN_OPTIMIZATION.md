**"District Manager" at ADP reads as a retail people-manager title to every recruiter outside the payroll industry.** Recruiters filling SaaS/AI/med-device seats search for: `Account Executive`, `quota`, `President's Club`, `full cycle`, `new logo`, `outside sales`, `Salesforce`. Your profile has to hit those tokens and instantly translate your title — without lying about it. Everything below serves that.

Your second unfair advantage: **you build your own tools** (the Python prospecting engine, the team scorecard, AI research briefs in this repo). You'd never brag about it, so the profile states it as plain fact — for AI and SaaS recruiters it's the single strongest differentiator, because it reads "AE who thinks like RevOps."

## 7. ALGORITHM / ACTIVITY — 30-DAY PLAYBOOK

**Goal:** raise your Social Selling Index (SSI) and surface in recruiter feeds/searches, in your voice — numbers, no hype.

**Week 0 — foundations (days 1–2):**
- Ship headline, About, Experience, Skills, Featured, and settings above.
- Request 5+ recommendations from a former manager and peers (FY25 leadership, teammates) referencing quota, rank, and the tools you built.
- Claim the vanity URL; check your SSI baseline at linkedin.com/sales/ssi.

**Connection strategy (~15–20 invites/day, under the ~100/week soft cap):**
- **Recruiters** at target companies: search `recruiter OR "talent acquisition"` filtered to SaaS / AI / medical-device companies + NYC. After they accept, send the DM template (with rank and quota $ filled in).
- **AEs and sales leaders** at those companies — 2nd-degree reach puts your content in recruiter feeds.
- Connect right after engaging with a person's post; accept rates jump.

**Posting cadence — 2 posts/week, understated, zero job-seeking signals:**

*Post 1 — the builder story (highest reach potential):*
> "I got tired of buying lead lists, so I built my own." → one plain sentence on what it does (pulls Google Maps + Yelp + NYC public records, dedupes, scores each lead on website signals, drafts outreach, exports to Salesforce) → one honest takeaway: the scoring model beats gut feel on which firms actually run payroll in-house. Sanitized repo link in comments. No CTA, no adjectives.

*Post 2 — a number, plainly stated:*
> "FY26, by the numbers." → 120% of quota. President's Club. #1 of [FILL IN: N] DMs (DM of the Year). 8 of 12 months at quota. → one line on what changed vs FY25 (self-sourcing discipline / the tooling) → what you're testing in FY27 (the referral channel). No "humbled/blessed."

*Post 3 — a lesson, not a flex:*
> "What promoting early actually taught me." → the un-glamorous habit: [FILL IN: specific — call volume, disqualifying fast, follow-up cadence] → one concrete example → "what's the boring habit that carries your number?"

**Engagement (daily, 10 min):**
- 3–5 substantive comments/day on posts from target-company AEs, sales leaders, recruiters (comments massively out-perform likes for reach).
- 1 repost/week with a one-line take.
- Reply to every comment on your own posts within the first hour — early velocity drives distribution.

**SSI drivers:** establish brand (profile + Featured + posts) · find the right people (targeted invites) · engage with insights (daily comments) · build relationships (senior connections, not just peers). Day-30 target: ~300+ relevant connections, 8 posts, SSI 70+. Consistency beats bursts.

## Honesty guardrails before publishing

1. **President's Club:** always tied to "ADP's 100% Club" (done in About + table) — the club you actually won, described in the language recruiters search.
2. **Rank denominators:** "#1 of N" only ships once N and scope (team/region/nation) are confirmed in writing — the brag book must back every number.
3. **Fill or delete every `[FILL IN]`** — empty brackets or invented figures fail a screen faster than modest real numbers.
4. **Stealth re-scan:** before publishing, re-read the final public profile for any "looking / open / exploring" language. The only places targeting appears: recruiters-only Open to Work, and private DMs.
