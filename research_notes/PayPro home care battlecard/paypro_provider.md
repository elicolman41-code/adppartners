# Which "PayPro" a 12-employee home care agency likely uses, and what it offers

Research date: 2026-10-02. Note: Firecrawl search hit its rate limit (HTTP 429) partway through, so coverage of BBB/Google reviews and contract terms is thin. Those gaps are stated below rather than filled in.

## Which PayPro entities exist, where they are, how big they are, and what platform each runs on

### Takeaway
At least four companies use the "PayPro" name. The strongest match for a home care agency is **Paypro Workforce Management (Paypro Corporation, payprocorp.com, Ronkonkoma NY)**. It is the only one with a dedicated home care page, and it runs on **UKG**, not isolved. Two cautions: (a) Vestwell's help center says a payroll provider called "PayPro" is now "powered by iSolved Direct (following an acquisition)", so a second PayPro-branded book of business on isolved may exist; and (b) PayPros, Inc. in Cocoa, FL is an isolved partner. **We cannot tell which one the prospect uses without asking.** Ask for a pay stub or the login URL. A `*.myisolved.com` address means isolved. A UKG/"HCMToGo" login means Paypro Corp.

### Cited Findings
- Paypro Workforce Management: HQ 115 Comac Street, Ronkonkoma NY 11779. Founded 1992 by a CPA. 51-200 staff. "UKG-powered platform" covering payroll, HR, time and attendance, benefits admin, and the HCMToGo mobile app. Claims 96%+ client retention and 250,000+ worksite employees. Serves small, mid-size and enterprise clients, including healthcare and field-based workforces — [DHRMap](https://www.dhrmap.com/companies/paypro)
- Software Advice and GetApp listings also describe the Paypro WFM solution as "powered by UKG" — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/bi/paypro-workforce-management-profile/)
- LinkedIn company page: "Paypro Workforce Management" (slug paypro-corporation) — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paypro-corporation)
- A provider called "PayPro" is listed on Vestwell as "now powered by iSolved Direct (following an acquisition)". It is unclear which PayPro this is or who acquired it. I found no press release confirming an isolved acquisition of any PayPro (unverified) — [Vestwell](https://support.vestwell.com/hc/en-us/articles/13522601223319-PayPro)
- PayPros, Inc.: 956 N Cocoa Blvd, Cocoa FL. Small-business payroll and HR. Its time and attendance is "100% native part of the iSolved solution", making it an isolved reseller or partner — [PayPros](https://www.payprosinc.com/), [SHRM vendor directory](https://vendordirectory.shrm.org/company/833125/paypros-inc)
- Pay Pros (Henderson NV) was acquired by G&A Partners (a PEO) in October 2019, so it is likely no longer a standalone brand — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/pay-pros)
- PayPro Global is a software and SaaS e-commerce merchant of record, not a payroll company. Rule it out — [PayPro Global](https://payproglobal.com/)
- Regional fit: Paypro Corp (Long Island NY) fits a Northeast prospect. PayPros Inc fits a Florida prospect. Location is a useful tiebreaker (my inference).

## What they charge (per run, PEPM, setup) for about 12 employees

### Takeaway
No public price list exists for any PayPro entity. Paypro Corp's own blog gives only industry ranges and says it offers "custom quote" pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Paypro's blog puts full-service payroll at "$30–$150/month + $5–$12/employee". It says W-2/1099 forms "may cost an additional $5 to $15 per form" in the industry, and that weekly payroll costs more than biweekly. It warns about setup fees, off-cycle fees and year-end fees, and claims Paypro is "transparent… without hidden fees" — [Paypro blog, Sep 2025](https://payprocorp.com/resources/blog/cost-of-payroll-services-for-small-businesses-and-how-to-choose-whats-right/)
- Illustrative range only, not a quote: applying that range to 12 employees gives about $90-$294/month. Home care often runs weekly payroll, which pushes the cost toward the high end. **Do not quote this to the prospect as PayPro's price.** Get their actual invoice.
- The Software Advice listing shows a placeholder "$0.01" price, which tells us nothing — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/bi/paypro-workforce-management-profile/)

## Whether they offer home care integrations or EVV

### Takeaway
Paypro Corp markets a home care page: varying pay rates, overtime, multiple shift types, caregiver certification tracking, multi-location support, and "integrate… with any agency management system". It **names no EVV capability and no specific integration (HHAeXchange, AxisCare, WellSky)**. I found nothing on travel-time pay, per-visit pay or EVV for any PayPro entity.

### Cited Findings
- Home care page claims: handles "varying pay rates, overtime, and a variety of different types of shifts". Tracks caregiver certifications and training. Manages multiple locations. Offers Excel export. Caregivers can self-serve pay stubs and W-2s. Claims "Robust Caregiver Data Integration… with any agency management system". Also claims compliance with "HIPAA and state-specific laws" and says leaks "will never be an issue", which is marketing language — [Paypro Home Care](https://payprocorp.com/who-we-help/home-care/)
- The page refers to "proprietary software" while third parties call it UKG-powered. These two descriptions don't fit together, so treat the vendor's wording with caution — same source vs. [DHRMap](https://www.dhrmap.com/companies/paypro)
- If the prospect is on an isolved-based PayPro: isolved publishes a home care case study (Right at Home) based on a scheduling integration and weekly payroll. This shows the platform can do it, not that the reseller has it set up — [isolved case study](https://www.isolvedhcm.com/customer/stories/dignified-home-care-services)
- Gap: I found no EVV aggregator listings and no HHAeXchange or AxisCare partner-directory entries for any PayPro. I could not search those directories because of the rate limit.

## Customer reviews and complaints (G2, Capterra, BBB, Google): strengths and weaknesses

### Takeaway
There are very few reviews. Paypro WFM has 4.7/5 from about 10 reviews on Software Advice. Reviewers praise its US-based support. The main complaint is that fixes depend on UKG's roadmap. I did not retrieve BBB or Google data.

### Cited Findings
- 4.7/5 from 10 reviews: ease of use 4.6, support 4.9, value 4.6, features 4.6. Reviewers praise the dedicated US-based support team — [Software Advice reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/bi/paypro-workforce-management-profile/reviews/)
- One reviewer complained about reporting errors and changes "that cannot be completed as they are not a priority for UKG", plus poor communication about updates — same source
- A G2 profile exists ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/paypro-workforce-management/reviews)). I did not review its content.
- BBB and Google: not retrieved because of the search rate limit. No findings.

## Contract terms and switching friction (notice periods, data export, year-end fees)

### Takeaway
I found no public contract terms for any PayPro. The only relevant signals: Paypro's own blog flags year-end form fees as an industry norm, and its home care page promotes Excel export. Ask the prospect for their agreement before claiming anything about switching costs.

### Cited Findings
- Paypro's blog lists setup fees, off-cycle fees, correction fees and W-2/1099 fees of $5-$15 per form as hidden costs to ask about — [Paypro blog](https://payprocorp.com/resources/blog/cost-of-payroll-services-for-small-businesses-and-how-to-choose-whats-right/)
- Its home care page advertises "quick and efficient" Excel export, which suggests data export may be straightforward — [Paypro Home Care](https://payprocorp.com/who-we-help/home-care/)
- If the prospect turns out to be on isolved Direct after an acquisition, that platform change could be an opening for a sales conversation. This is my speculation and has not been checked with the prospect — [Vestwell](https://support.vestwell.com/hc/en-us/articles/13522601223319-PayPro)
- Gap: I found no published notice periods, termination fees or data-export policies.
