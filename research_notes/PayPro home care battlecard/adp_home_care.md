# ADP RUN capabilities for small home care agencies (~12 employees, W-2 + 1099) vs. a local bureau like PayPro

Research date: 2026-10-02. The research hit search rate limits partway through, so some items below come only from search-result summaries and are marked "(snippet-level)". Verify those before quoting them to a prospect.

## Which home care software integrates with ADP RUN / Workforce Now (ADP Marketplace)?

### Takeaway
Home care platforms do NOT reliably offer a direct, API-level integration with RUN. HHAeXchange lists ADP as a partner. AxisCare's own ADP page says there is no direct API integration today. AlayaCare and WellSky (ClearCare) export payroll files rather than offering a confirmed native RUN connector. Do not claim "plug-and-play with your EMR." Position it as "your scheduling/EVV system exports approved hours, and ADP's AI timesheet import pulls them into RUN."

### Cited Findings
- **HHAeXchange** has a Partner Connect page for ADP. Its blog says "Our integration with ADP ensures that the hours tracked within HHAeXchange are input properly into ADP." — [HHAeXchange partner page](https://www.hhaexchange.com/partner-connect/adp); [HHAeXchange blog](https://www.hhaexchange.com/blog/make-caregivers-a-top-priority-by-streamlining-your-hr-and-payroll). The page is dated 2023 and does not say whether the connector is for RUN or Workforce Now.
- **AxisCare** lists ADP in its integration marketplace, but the page states there is currently no direct API integration with ADP and that AxisCare is "exploring future possibilities" (page modified Nov 2025) — [AxisCare](https://axiscare.com/integration-marketplace/adp/)
- **AlayaCare** approves timesheets, applies base and overtime rates, and "export[s] gross payroll information to a payroll processing system." One user review said there was no option for ADP Vantage (snippet-level) — [AlayaCare](https://alayacare.com/platform/overview/optimize-payroll-and-hr-functions/); [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/healthcare-pharmaceuticals-software/a/alayacare/)
- **WellSky Personal Care (formerly ClearCare)**: the only payroll integration that turned up was a WellSky–Netchex integration, and no ADP-specific page was found — [Netchex](https://netchex.com/blog/wellsky-netchex-integration/)
- **Homecare Pro** (a compliance and onboarding tool) advertises an ADP integration — [Homecare Pro](https://www.homecarepro.io/adp)
- **Hireology** (caregiver recruiting) has an ADP Marketplace app covering hiring, onboarding and payroll sync with ADP Workforce Now — [ADP Marketplace](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/171186/hireology/features)
- **All RUN tiers** include "ADP Assist smart timesheet import," which uses AI to import hours "from the timesheet file you already use today." This is the realistic bridge from any home care EMR export — [ADP packages](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/payroll/payroll-for-1-49-employees/payroll-packages.aspx)
- **ADP Marketplace time apps for RUN** include ADP Timekeeping for RUN, Timeero, OnTheClock and InfiniTime — [ADP Timekeeping for RUN](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/325308/adp-timekeeping-for-run-powered-by-adp/features); [Timeero](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/442961/Timeero%20for%20RUN%20Powered%20by%20ADP%C2%AE/features); [OnTheClock](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/416781/ontheclock-time-tracking-system-for-run-powered-by-adp/features)

### Inferences
- EVV for Medicaid visits will almost certainly stay in the agency's EMR or the state aggregator (HHAeXchange, Sandata and others). ADP time tools are not EVV systems. Pitch ADP as the payroll and tax layer behind EVV, not as a replacement for it.
- Per-visit pay and travel time between clients are normally calculated in the EMR and exported. RUN then pays the multiple earnings codes and rates it receives.

### Gaps
- No ADP Marketplace listing was confirmed for AxisCare, WellSky, AlayaCare, CareTime or ClearCare. The planned searches were blocked by rate limits. Have Eli check apps.adp.com directly.
- CareTime: no information found.
- The ADP-side documentation does not say how many pay rates per employee RUN supports or whether it has a native "per-visit" earning type.

## What RUN package tiers include what (Essential / Enhanced / Complete / HR Pro)?

### Takeaway
All four tiers include tax filing with ADP's penalty guarantee, 1099 forms, onboarding, background checks, Wisely Direct, the GL interface and 24/7 support. Enhanced adds SUI management, garnishments, job costing, labor-law posters, e-I-9 and ZipRecruiter. Complete adds the HR HelpDesk, handbook wizard and HR forms. HR Pro adds the applicant tracking system, learning management system, harassment training, enhanced HR support and legal assistance. Time & Attendance, workers' comp (including pay-as-you-go), health insurance and retirement are add-ons at every tier. ADP does not publish prices.

### Cited Findings
- **All tiers:** payroll by computer, mobile app or phone; direct deposit; 24/7 phone and chat support; reporting; GL export (QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, Creative Solutions); new-hire reporting; W-2 and 1099 creation and IRS e-file ("additional fee"); tax filing; payroll delivery; ADP Assist (AI assistant, payroll inspector, smart timesheet import); multi-company; multi-jurisdiction; Wisely Direct card; bill pay and invoicing; Employee Access; new-hire onboarding (W-4, direct deposit); employee discounts; background checks (single-county, SSN validation, criminal history) — [ADP packages](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/payroll/payroll-for-1-49-employees/payroll-packages.aspx)
- **Tax guarantee:** ADP will "calculate, file, deposit and reconcile your payroll taxes… respond to inquiries from taxing agencies… if we make a tax filing error, we pay the resulting fines or penalties" — [ADP packages](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/payroll/payroll-for-1-49-employees/payroll-packages.aspx)
- **Enhanced adds:** check signing and security (10 fraud features), SUI management (audits the SUI rate and handles state correspondence), garnishment payment service, job costing, labor law posters, ZipRecruiter, electronic I-9 — same source
- **Complete adds:** HR HelpDesk (phone and email HR professionals), Employee Handbook Wizard, salary benchmarks, HR forms and documents, HR training and toolkits — same source
- **HR Pro adds:** enhanced HR HelpDesk, CareerPlug applicant tracking, learning management system, sexual harassment prevention training, Upnetic legal assistance — same source. "HR Pro" is also listed as a Marketplace app — [ADP Marketplace](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/292428/adp-hr-pro)
- **Add-ons at every tier:** Time & Attendance, Retirement, Workers' Compensation ("simplified insurance and pay-as-you-go solutions," sold through ADP Insurance Agency, which is licensed in 50 states), Health Insurance — same source
- **1099-only option:** RUN can pay 1099 contractors "even if you don't pay W-2 employees." Contractor pricing is a monthly base plus a per-contractor fee, plus year-end fees — same source
- **Contract terms:** "You don't have to sign a contract, and services can be terminated at any time." Pricing depends on payroll frequency, headcount and add-ons; off-cycle runs cost extra. Current promotion: 3 months free (ends 10/22) — same source
- **Scale:** "over 900,000 small business clients" — same source
- **Pricing:** ADP's small-business page says "as low as $39 per month, plus $5 per employee" — [ADP 1-49](https://www.adp.com/who-we-serve/by-business-size/1-49-employees.aspx). That is a marketing starting price. Real quotes are custom: "ADP does not publish a rate card" — [PayrollSoftwareGuide](https://payrollsoftwareguide.com/reviews/adp-run/)
- **Tier conflict:** Tech.co lists background checks under Essential. ADP's own page lists them in all tiers, and the Enhanced blurb repeats "background checks" — [Tech.co](https://tech.co/hr-software/adp-pricing-guide). Forbes says Time & Attendance is an add-on even in HR Pro, which matches ADP — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/advisor/business/software/adp-payroll-pricing/)

### Inferences
- For a 12-person home care agency, the realistic fit is **Enhanced + Time & Attendance + pay-as-you-go workers' comp**. SUI management and e-I-9 matter in high-turnover caregiver staffing. **Complete** becomes the better fit if the owner has no HR person, because of the HelpDesk and handbook.
- Pay-as-you-go workers' comp helps cash flow in home care, where premiums are high. It is sold through ADP's insurance agency, so do not quote rates.

### Gaps
- No public tier prices. Do not quote any beyond ADP's own "$39 + $5" starting line.
- Not confirmed: ADP Workforce Now home-health-specific features, ADP's breakpoint for moving a client from RUN to WFN, and the "HR411" brand name (no current source found; ADP now markets this as HR HelpDesk).

## Strongest differentiators vs. a small local bureau (e.g., PayPro), and complaints to pre-empt

### Takeaway
ADP's edge is breadth in one platform: tax penalty guarantee, SUI management, built-in background checks and onboarding, pay-as-you-go workers' comp, Wisely/EWA options for hourly caregivers, a marketplace of time and EWA apps, 24/7 support, AI error-checking and no long-term contract. ADP's weaknesses are service consistency, opaque pricing, fees that rise over time, and the lack of a native connector to most home care EMRs. A local bureau will sell "you get a person who knows you." Answer with ADP's partner-rep relationship and the no-contract terms.

### Cited Findings
- **Caregiver pay access:** Wisely Direct is in all tiers at no employer cost, with nothing for the employer to implement — [ADP packages](https://www.adp.com/what-we-offer/payroll/payroll-for-1-49-employees/payroll-packages.aspx). Earned wage access apps on the Marketplace include Payactiv for RUN (syncs hours and posts deductions back), Tapcheck for RUN, and DailyPay Preferred for Wisely (up to 100% of earned wages before payday) — [Payactiv](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/224873/payactiv-for-run-powered-by-adp/features); [Tapcheck](https://apps.adp.com/en-us/apps/328509/tapcheck-connector-for-run-powered-by-adp/features); [DailyPay](https://apps.adp.com/en-us/apps/592008/dailypay-preferred-for-wisely-by-adp/features)
- **Time tracking (snippet-level):** the ADP Time Kiosk app turns an iPad or Android tablet into a time clock using a badge number or optional biometric/facial recognition. ADP time tools offer mobile punch with geofencing and a GPS breadcrumb trail, timecard approval, PTO accruals and scheduling — [ADP Time Kiosk, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1455253414); [ADP Timekeeping for RUN](https://apps.adp.com/en-US/apps/325308/adp-timekeeping-for-run-powered-by-adp/features)
- **Complaints:** Trustpilot reviewers cite poor service, login problems, payroll errors, and trouble getting tax documents and fees resolved. One post cited about 910 BBB complaints and a 1.05-star rating (that is a LinkedIn post, not a primary source) — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/adp.com); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/pallifrone_i-researched-adp-i-did-not-expect-it-to-activity-7493377994942668800-9fy1)
- **Complaints:** "ADP is reliable until it isn't… fixes can take time and involve multiple handoffs" — [Reddit r/Payroll](https://www.reddit.com/r/Payroll/comments/1poozhe/what_is_your_experience_with_adp_opinion_needed/)
- **Complaints:** quotes are custom, and mid-contract price increases are a "recurring complaint" — [PayrollSoftwareGuide](https://payrollsoftwareguide.com/reviews/adp-run/)
- ADP's own response is to judge reviews from payroll and HR users rather than from employees — [ADP article](https://www.adp.com/resources/articles-and-insights/articles/c/customer-service-navigating-reviews-and-recommendations-for-small-bus-payroll-and-hr-systems.aspx)

### Inferences
- **Pre-empt objections:**
  1. Service: Eli is the named contact, and support is available 24/7.
  2. Price creep: put pricing in writing and note there is no contract.
  3. Integration: show the EMR export going through smart timesheet import during the demo instead of promising a connector.
  4. 1099 and year-end fees: disclose them up front, since W-2/1099 forms carry an "additional fee."
- **Home care compliance angle:** caregiver misclassification (1099 vs. W-2) and overtime/travel-time rules (FLSA home care rule) are real risks. The HR HelpDesk (Complete and above) is where to route those questions. Do not claim ADP guarantees classification or wage-hour compliance.
- **Avoid unsupported claims:** never say ADP is "cheaper" than PayPro. No PayPro pricing was researched, and ADP's prices are not public.

### Gaps
- No PayPro-specific data (out of scope; another researcher covers it).
- No primary ADP security or SOC report details were collected.
- No G2 or Capterra ratings were pulled because of rate limits.
- No caregiver-specific ADP case study was found.
