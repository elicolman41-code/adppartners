## Four compliance risks to put on the table

**1099 caregivers are the headline risk.** DOL decides employee status on economic reality ([DOL WHD](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification)), and personal care work is rarely done by genuinely independent businesses ([NELP](https://www.nelp.org/insights-research/independent-contractor-classification-in-home-care/)). Damages often come doubled. Home Healthcare Corp. paid **$1.6M, half back wages and half liquidated damages, to 546 workers** ([HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/home-healthcare-company-to-pay-16m-to-settle-dol-misclassification-claim/596440/)). California settled a **$10M** home care misclassification case under the ABC test ([Miller Shah](https://millershah.com/blog/california-home-care-worker-misclassification/)). Office staff are exposed as well: an Ohio agency paid **$975K** over managers wrongly classified as exempt ([JD Supra](https://www.jdsupra.com/topics/misclassification/wage-and-hour/home-health-care/)). *Unverified, for her CPA to confirm:* paying similar workers on both W-2 and 1099 likely removes Section 530 safe-harbor relief, and the IRS VCSP program is a possible clean-up route.

**The companionship exemption is still in limbo, so do not tell her overtime went away.** DOL proposed letting third-party agencies claim the exemption again on **July 2, 2025** ([Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/07/02/2025-12316/application-of-the-fair-labor-standards-act-to-domestic-service)) and paused federal enforcement on **July 25, 2025** (FAB 2025-4). No final rule had been found as of the August 2026 agenda (RIN 1235-AA51) ([Littler](https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/dol-proposes-rule-reinstate-companionship-live-exemptions-minimum-wage-and); [FR agenda](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/14/2026-16599/agenda-of-regulations)). Private lawsuits remain open. In April 2026 the Sixth Circuit left WiCare Home Care with a **$1.1M** loss after upholding the 2013 rule ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/daily-labor-report/home-health-care-company-stuck-with-1-1-million-wage-case-loss)). **Check federalregister.gov before the meeting.**

**Overtime and travel time.** *Unverified, from standard DOL guidance:* travel between clients counts as paid work time, overtime is calculated on total hours across all clients, and per-visit pay must still reach minimum wage and roll into the regular rate.

**State rules.** These depend on Healthify's state, so confirm it. In NY, the home care minimum wage for NYC, Long Island and Westchester rises to **$19.65 on January 1, 2026** ([Rivkin Radler](https://www.rivkinradler.com/publications/ny-amends-home-care-worker-minimum-wage/)), and Medicaid-funded agencies there are subject to **Wage Parity** total-compensation rules ([NY DOL](https://dol.ny.gov/home-health-care-aides-and-wage-parity)). Colorado sets a **$17/hr** base wage for direct care workers in home and community-based services ([CO HCPF](https://hcpf.colorado.gov/direct-care-workforce-base-wage)). The federal Medicaid 80/20 pass-through rule (effective 2030) may be revised, so do not present it as a fixed deadline ([LeadingAge](https://leadingage.org/cms-finalizes-medicaid-access-rule-with-80-20-wage-pass-through/); [Paradigm](https://www.paradigmseniors.com/blog/cms-signals-potential-reversal-of-medicaids-80-20-rule-what-home-care-providers-should-know)).

## Discovery questions and objection handling

| Discovery question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| What's the URL you log into PayPro with? Can I see a stub? | Tells you which PayPro and platform (UKG or isolved) |
| Which state(s)? Any Medicaid clients? | State wage rules, Wage Parity, EVV |
| What scheduling/EVV system do you use, and how do hours get into payroll today? | The integration story; manual re-keying is your opening |
| How are the 1099 caregivers different from the W-2 ones in schedule, clients and supervision? | Surfaces misclassification risk without accusing her |
| Do caregivers see multiple clients a day? How is drive time between them paid? | Travel time and overtime exposure |
| Weekly or biweekly? Any off-cycle runs? | Cost drivers on both sides |
| Who handles HR questions and your workers' comp policy? | Need for HR HelpDesk and pay-as-you-go WC |
| Does your CPA review payroll? Who is it? | Brings in Eli's CPA-partner angle |
| What does your PayPro agreement say about notice and termination? What's on last month's invoice? | Switching friction and a real price comparison |

| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| "PayPro knows us; ADP is a call center." | Fair point; their reviews are strong. You'd have me as your named rep plus 24/7 support, and there's no contract, so you aren't locked in if it doesn't work. |
| "ADP is more expensive." | Maybe. I won't guess. Let me quote it against your actual invoice, including year-end form fees on both sides. |
| "Does it plug into my EMR?" | Not natively, as far as I can confirm. Your system exports approved hours and RUN's timesheet import brings them in. Let's test it with your real export. |
| "My 1099s are fine; everyone does it." | That's a question for your CPA or attorney, not me. What I can do is make a W-2 transition clean: onboarding, e-I-9, workers' comp and SUI handling. |
| "Overtime rules for home care are going away." | The federal rule is proposed, not final, and private suits are still being won. State law may require overtime regardless. Worth confirming before changing pay. |
| "Switching mid-year is a pain." | Understood. Let's look at your agreement's notice terms and pick a clean start date. |
