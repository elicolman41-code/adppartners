NYON FY27 BEYOND PAYROLL SCORECARD
====================================

HOW TO USE
----------
1. Open NYON-FY27-Beyond-Payroll-Scorecard.html in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
   No internet connection required. Nothing is installed. Nothing is sent anywhere.

2. First launch: a welcome screen asks for your name. Enter it and click "Get Started."
   Your data is saved locally on this device only.

FEATURES
--------
• ASSOCIATE MANAGER (top bar)
  - Switch between associates using the dropdown.
  - Click "Save" at any time to manually save your data.
  - Click "+ Add New Associate" to create a new slot.
  - Click "Delete" to remove an associate and all their data.

• MONTHLY TABS
  - Click any month tab (JUL–JUN) above the scorecard grid to jump to that month.
  - The active column is highlighted. Tab/Enter moves down the column; Shift+Tab moves up.

• SMART NUMBER ENTRY
  - Type "1.2" to enter $1,200. Type "5.1" for $5,100.
  - Whole numbers are literal: "1200" = $1,200.
  - A live preview appears at the bottom of the screen as you type.

• FY26 vs FY27 TAB
  - Click the "FY26 vs FY27" tab to compare year-over-year performance.
  - Enter FY26 goals and actuals for each metric.
  - The delta column shows FY27 YTD vs FY26 actual (green = ahead, red = behind).
  - All comparison data saves per associate.

• PRINT / PDF
  - Click "Print / PDF" to print or save as PDF. The header, cards, grid, and
    revenue summary are all print-optimized (landscape layout).

DISTRIBUTION
------------
Send this single HTML file to each associate. They open it in their browser,
enter their name on first launch, and start tracking. Their data stays on their
own device. You can collect updated copies of the file to review progress.

For manager use: add each team member as a separate associate in your own copy.
