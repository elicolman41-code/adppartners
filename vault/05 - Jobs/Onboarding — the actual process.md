# Onboarding — Eli's actual process
*From Eli, 2026-09-25. This replaces any longer pre-flight list for explaining the process to a partner.*

## The steps
1. **SS-4** from the client. That's the whole information ask.
2. **Eli creates the account.**
3. **Eli sends the e-sign.**
4. **Walk through digital onboarding together.** About 20 minutes on a call.
5. **Account is ready in a day or two** after digital onboarding is complete.
6. **Cash payroll turned on** first.
7. **After the first payroll, Run & Done turned on.**

## The only two things needed from the client
- Business bank information
- Employee information

## Tax IDs
- Client applies for any state tax IDs.
- If they can't, Eli helps them apply and gives exact directions.

## Notes
- The SS-4 carries legal name, trade name, EIN, address, responsible party, entity type, start date, fiscal year end, expected employees. One document covers the company block.
- Don't over-explain this to a partner. It is short on purpose.
