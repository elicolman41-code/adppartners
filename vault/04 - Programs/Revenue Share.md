# Revenue Share

ADP's accountant referral program.

- Tiers: 1–4 clients = 25% · 5–9 = 50% · 10+ = 75%
- **Retroactive**: crossing a tier re-rates ALL clients (hit 5 → first four bump to 50%)
- Residuals: 10% in years 2–5 at 5+ clients
- Pays most on **January starts** — plan Q4 conversations accordingly

Example math (Mohammed, 6 clients @ $1,200): 50% = $3,600/yr + $720/yr residuals yrs 2–5 = ~$6,480 over five years.
