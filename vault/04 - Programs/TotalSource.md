# TotalSource (PEO)

ADP's PEO — co-employment model. Largest PEO in the nation.

- Co-employment = contractual split: ADP takes payroll/tax filing under its FEIN, W-2s, benefit plan sponsorship, workers' comp. Client keeps hiring/firing/supervision.
- NY 2027 small group renewal requests: 25.7% avg, UHC 42.1%, Oxford 34.5% (REQUESTED, not approved — DFS historically cuts ~half)
- TotalSource trend ~9% for certain clients — never quote as guarantee; needs internal source doc
- Broker program: 1.4x annualized revenue, 90-day retention, ring-fencing. 20-unit threshold on the pilot.
