# Paychex billing — how 401(k) admin is invoiced
*Fable, 2026-09-21. From two real client document sets + 4 Opus research agents, checked.*

## Verified from documents
- **Payroll invoice** = "Notice of Automatic Payment", one per payroll run, keyed to check date. Quality Care (Flex Select, bi-weekly): Flex Select $142 / 24 transactions, WC service $3, time-off $3, postage $11.31, discount -$72.13 → $87.18. Walters (HR Pro, bi-weekly): one line "Paychex HR Pro (Active Employees)" qty 3, $896.70 − $394.55 = $502.15.
- **401(k) admin** = separate MONTHLY "Retirement Services" ACH statement from the 401k Client Web portal (Retirement Services → Billing Statements). Quality Care: $150 base + $6/participant (was $135 + $5 through Feb 2026, raised 3/1/26 with retro true-up in April). Plan doc fee $30 taken from participant assets. Billed participant count (7) ≠ balances (44); matched "actively contributing 20%" of 35 — inference.
- A Paychex client WITH a funded 401(k) shows NO 401(k) line on the payroll invoice. So a blank payroll invoice proves nothing about whether a 401(k) fee exists.
- Walters trust statement footnote: "This does not include administration or annual account fees paid from your bank account. Refer to your Invoice."

## Not settled: does HR Pro bundle it?
- Capterra + FindHR list HR Pro as including "Retirement Plan (Recordkeeping and Administration)". Paychex page: "HR Pro — HR Guidance, Payroll, Safety, 401(k)". Paychex 408(b)(2) defines "Retirement Services Included Clients" whose admin/per-participant/setup fees are inside the bundle fee — but they STILL pay an Annual Account Fee.
- Gusto's guide: retirement "available separately and through some HR configurations."
- Whether the fee is its own ACH is a Multi-Debit vs Single-Debit election in the Retirement Services Agreement, not a package feature.
- No public 2025–26 Paychex invoicing change found. Paychex publishes no pricing; the $135→$150 increase is invoice-only.

## Exit costs to keep in the back pocket (secondary sources, don't quote as fact)
$1,500 plan transfer fee; discontinuance 0.50–1.50% of assets inside a 5-year term; reviewers report $1,500–$3,000 termination fees.

## The one ask that settles any account
Screenshot of the client's 401k portal **Billing Statements** page, or the 408(b)(2) cover page (says whether they're a Retirement Services Included Client).

## Update 2026-09-21 — the HRS Billing Statement (third document type)
Seen for Onchain + Spencer Thwaytes (Stephen's book, plans opened 2026). Issuer "Human Resource Services, 1175 John St, West Henrietta NY", 1-800-472-0072. Monthly, ACH ~10 days after statement. Lines: 3(16) Plan Administration $25 · Monthly Recordkeeping $150 · Participant $6 each · **SET UP $111.09/mo, "INSTALLMENT $3999.00"** = $3,999 financed over 36 months · Annual Account Fee ~70 bps from participant assets. So a brand-new small Paychex 401(k) costs ~$3,400–3,700/yr in hard fees, on top of the payroll invoice. Quality Care (older plan) had no setup line and no 3(16) line — the $25 3(16) + $3,999 setup look like the 2026 new-plan package.

## Setup fee + exit fees — research 2026-09-21 (2 Opus agents, snippet-level, fetches blocked)
- Setup fee is REAL and is demanded at cancellation: r/Payroll 1id2pn8 (Jan 2025): "$1,500 termination fee and $1,400 left to the 'set up' fee. There isn't so much as $0.01 in our 401k." BBB: $4,950 setup disputed via bank + rescission demand.
- Paychex books setup as deferred revenue over 3–4 yrs (10-Q) → the 36 installments are revenue recognition, not a courtesy.
- $1,500 = "Plan Transfer Fee", Paychex's own words (BenefitsLink): applies to a client "who transfers its plan recordkeeping to a new service provider and who is not currently/does not continue to process payroll with Paychex." WAIVED if client keeps Paychex payroll. Stephen's clients are leaving payroll → fee applies.
- 408(b)(2) template lists Setup Fee and Plan Termination Fee with NO amount → the amount lives on the client-specific schedule. Non-disclosure argument only works if the client's own signed schedule is blank.
- ZERO public accounts of a SETUP fee being waived. Only documented relief: BBB complaint → credit/refund on a termination fee. Eli's "one CPA got it waived" = single-source hearsay; get that CPA's credit memo.
- LEVER: Paychex Accountant Partner Program promises referred clients "one month of free Paychex payroll, HR, and retirement services" and "a six-month, money-back satisfaction guarantee for payroll and retirement services." All three plans opened May–Aug 2026 → inside 6 months. If Stephen referred them via Partner Pro, invoke the guarantee, not a favor.
- SECURE 2.0 §45E credit covers setup costs but is capped at greater of $500 or $250/NHCE (max $5K) and is a credit against tax owed — for a 3-EE shop ~$750/yr. Don't let anyone say "the credit covers it."
- Relief default is an ACCOUNT CREDIT. Ask for ACH refund in writing.
