# Grid (Payroll Book Buyout)

Acquisition program for firms running payroll in-house.

- Pays a **multiple of annualized ADP revenue** per client onboarded (confirm multiplier per deal — 2x vs 2.75x has been ambiguous; ALWAYS verify before quoting)
- Retention conditions + installment payouts apply — never let a partner discover this in the paperwork
- Ideal profile: accountant processing payroll themselves at break-even (Slava: 80 clients, Black Ink: 300+)
- Lesson learned: quoted Steven $46K when real number was ~$32K at 2x. Verify multiplier FIRST.
## Retention + payout mechanics (Eli, 2026-09-23)
- All clients submitted **together as one list**. Submission cannot be staggered.
- Once approved, roughly **one month to six weeks** to get them all started. Starts CAN be staggered.
- Each converted client must stay **90 days**.
- **135 days** after, ADP pays **2x annual revenue** of every client that cleared the 90 days.
- Partial conversion is fine: submit 10, six convert → paid on the six.
- Worked example Eli uses: 20 clients x $2,000 annual revenue = $40,000 → **$80,000 at 135 days**.
