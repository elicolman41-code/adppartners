## Michigan UIA — adding ADP as TPA (from ADP implementation, 2026-09-24)
- **ADP TPA ID: 50000003** — name **ADP Tax Services, Inc**. (The 50000018 seen on a client screen is NOT ADP.)
- Prerequisite: the **state unemployment account must already be established**. TPA authorization does not create the account.
- Path: launch **MiUI** from the client's **MiLogin for Business** account → TPA Authorization (Non-WOTC path).
- Roles to confirm/assign:
  - Submit Employment and Wage Detail
  - Edit Tax Payments Update and Submit
  - View Rate Notice and Voluntary Contribution  ← add this one, often missed
- Michigan rules: TPA begin date = first day of a calendar quarter, end date = last day of one. Two TPAs cannot both hold Edit/Submit on the same role.
