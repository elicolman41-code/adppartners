# Platinum

Per-client payout = 1x annual ADP revenue for onboarded clients.

- Amberstone example: 7 clients, $14,100 annual revenue → $14,100 payout ($1,100 Oct 2026, $13,000 Apr 2027)
- Payouts follow client-specific schedules; subject to retention
- All figures are SCHEDULED not paid until they land — label accordingly on any doc a CPA will read.
