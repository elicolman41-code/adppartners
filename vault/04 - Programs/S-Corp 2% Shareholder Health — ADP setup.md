# S-Corp >2% Shareholder Health Insurance — ADP setup (reusable)

Researched 2026-09-14 for Stephen / Innovative Advisors (Backfin LLC). He said it applies to several other clients — keep this current.

## The answer (cleared review)
- **ADP RUN earning: "S-Corp 2% Medical Plan"** — Fringe Benefit (non-cash/memo). Box 1 YES · Boxes 3/5 NO · FUTA NO · Box 14 standard label.
- Setup path (ADP-hosted Getting Started S-Corp PDF): Settings → Payroll → Earnings & Deductions → Add Earning → Fringe Benefit → S-Corp 2% Medical Plan → flag the >2% shareholder.
- **Year-end lump sum is fine and is ADP's own suggestion** ("owners may consider entering premiums once per year in December").
- **Deadline: BEFORE the final scheduled payroll of the year.** Miss it → off-cycle dated 12/31, penalty exposure if after quarter close. Jan 31 W-2 deadline is NOT the real deadline.
- Reimbursed individual policy QUALIFIES under Notice 2008-1 if the S-corp reimburses AND reports on W-2. Policy in owner's name is fine. The W-2 entry is the qualifying act — skip it and the owner loses the Sec. 162(l) deduction.

## NOT SETTLED — never state as fact
- **FITW on a no-cash year-end lump sum.** Earning is FIT-taxable. Pub 15-B says "subject to federal income tax withholding"; practitioners run it as memo w/ withholding blocked (Rev. Rul. 91-26 guaranteed-payment analogy) and the owner trues up. How RUN actually handles negative net: UNCONFIRMED from ADP sources. → Ask implementation.
- **NY SUI wage base and NY PFL/DBL wage base** treatment of this earning: UNVERIFIED. NY income tax withholding follows federal wages (tax.ny.gov, summary-level only).

## ⚠️ THE TRAP (raise it every time)
Reimbursing an INDIVIDUAL policy is ACA-safe ONLY under the one-participant exemption (plan with fewer than 2 current-employee participants). **The moment the S-corp has a second W-2 employee, it's a non-compliant employer payment plan — Sec. 4980D, $100/day/employee.** Notice 2015-17 relief EXPIRED end of 2015. Fix for multi-employee clients: ICHRA or QSEHRA (not researched — confirm before recommending).
