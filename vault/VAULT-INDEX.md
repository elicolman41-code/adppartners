## What converts for Eli (learned)
- Community-embedded multiservice firms (tax + notary + insurance + real estate) — Caribbean corridor, Russian-speaking Sheepshead Bay, Boro Park
- Newly-opened solos, especially spin-offs from bigger firms — no incumbent vendor, hungry
- Chamber-of-commerce ribbon cuttings = best nationwide discovery signal
- HARDEST to close: elite-pedigree boutiques (ex-Big-4/Wall Street) — they have options and negotiate everything

## Voice & rules for outreach (learned the hard way)
- Short. Plain. No "I hope this finds you well," no exclamation marks, not salesy or robotic
- Concede impossible prices honestly and early — credibility is the asset
- Never mock or reference a prospect's typos. Never pressure after a no — door-open beats re-ask
- ESL contacts: short words, short sentences, no idioms
- Never promise savings/outcomes in writing; label requested rates as REQUESTED; footnote performance figures
- Texts: tiny. One ask. Question they can answer in one line
