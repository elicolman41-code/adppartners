## Things to know going in (don't lead with these — listen first)
- **1099 caregivers are a known risk area in home care.** When the agency sets schedules, assigns patients and controls how the work is done, the IRS and the Department of Labor generally view those workers as employees. Ask the questions above, note the answers, and flag to his accountant if it looks off. Don't give him a legal opinion.
- **Home care overtime.** Since 2015, third-party home care agencies generally have to pay minimum wage and overtime to aides, including travel time between clients. Relevant if caregivers do long weeks or multiple visits a day.
- **EVV.** If they bill Medicaid, they likely need electronic visit verification. Ask how they're handling it today.
- These are flags to note, not claims to make. Verify anything state-specific before saying it.
