What we need from you

  • Business bank account information — account number and routing number, plus one of: a recent bank statement, a voided check, or a screenshot of your online banking page.
  • Confirm your employee list. We pull names, pay rates and withholding from your current payroll reports, so you only need to confirm it is right and tell us about anyone missing.
  • Legal business name, address and EIN exactly as the IRS has them — your SS-4 or IRS confirmation letter is the cleanest way to send this. Tax filings reject on a name mismatch.
  • State tax account numbers — withholding and unemployment — plus your current unemployment rate, for every state where you have an employee.
  • Pay dates and pay period start and end dates through the end of the year. Your schedule is semi monthly.
  • Maryland withholding account number. The Paychex report shows it as "Applied For" — confirm whether it has been issued.
  • Confirm the current employee count and states. The Paychex Q2 report shows 5 employees across DC, Kentucky, Maryland and Ohio, plus Covington City and Kenton County local taxes.
  • Your name, email and mobile number for the account contact.

What you do not need to do

  • You do not need to cancel your current provider. Tell us your last payroll date and we handle the timing so no pay period gets run twice and none gets missed.
  • You do not need to gather tax filings or balances — we pull those from your current payroll reports.
  • You do not need to chase anything internally at ADP. Notices, problems, questions — they come to me.
