What I need from each client

  • SS-4. That one document has almost everything — legal name, EIN, address, entity type. If they don’t have it handy, the tax ID, legal business name and business address will do.
  • Business bank information.
  • Employee information. Names, pay rates, direct deposit.
Bank proof is required for every client. A voided check, a bank letter, or a screenshot of the account. This has to come from the client directly, not from you. It is the one item that will hold up an account if it’s missing.

How onboarding runs

  • You send the SS-4.
  • I create the account and send the e-sign.
  • We walk through digital onboarding together, about twenty minutes.
  • Account is ready in a day or two.
  • Cash payroll goes on first. After the first payroll we turn on RUN & Done.

State tax IDs
